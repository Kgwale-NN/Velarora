VELARORA — REFINED PRESENTATION

WHAT CHANGED

14 slides with shorter, more natural wording. Black and white slide backgrounds, an elegant serif font and nine Pexels photographs. The architecture, workflow and use-case slides use editable diagrams. The schedule-test slide uses the actual saved test times rather than a decorative stock image. The separate speaking guide contains the fuller explanation.

FONT

The reference website, Passalacqua, declares Canela Thin and Canela Light in its public stylesheet. The screenshot is consistent with that style. A desktop Canela font file was not available in this project. This deck uses Cormorant Garamond as a close alternative, not an exact font match.

Source checked: https://www.passalacqua.it/en/homepage

The included font ZIP contains Cormorant Garamond Regular, Medium, SemiBold and Italic plus its Open Font License. On the presentation laptop:

1. Extract Velarora-Fonts.zip.
2. Select the four .ttf files.
3. Right-click and choose Install. On Windows 11 you may need Show more options first.
4. Close and reopen PowerPoint.
5. Open Velarora-Refined-v2.pptx and press F5.

The intended look depends on installing the font. The deck does not embed font binaries. It uses native PowerPoint fades and title entrances. Rehearse in the laptop's PowerPoint because some web viewers handle fonts and animation differently.

HOW TO FIND A REPLACEMENT PHOTO

1. Open https://www.pexels.com/.
2. Copy the search phrase for the slide you want to change.
3. Choose a photo with a clear subject and space for cropping.
4. Download it from its original Pexels page.
5. In PowerPoint, right-click the existing picture and choose Change Picture > From a File.
6. Use Picture Format > Crop to position the subject clearly.

Pexels licence: https://www.pexels.com/license/
Check the current licence and the individual photo page when downloading or reusing a photo. Keep these credits with your project. A stock photo of a child or teacher is an illustration of classroom activity, not an accusation or evidence about that person.
